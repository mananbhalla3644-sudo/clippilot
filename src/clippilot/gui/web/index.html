What should ClipPilot do?

Describe the job in plain English. It edits video with a real ffmpeg engine and drives Windows apps with the mouse and keyboard.

  • “cut the first 12 seconds off D:\raw.mp4, make it vertical, add captions”
  • “find the best 30 seconds in my podcast recording and burn in subtitles”
  • “export it and open the result in CapCut”

Move the mouse into the top-left corner at any time to abort instantly.
